# Why Does Open Innovation Matter? — short answer for the dev.to contest form

> Paste-ready text. Touches nothing else; purely a copy-and-paste answer.

---

**Open innovation matters to me because it lets the platform get out of the way.**

I use Legion (github.com/MyLegionOnline/legion-hub) for three things: cloud development, multi-agent capability, and a git-flow that keeps the whole project in order. None of those tell me which LLM I have to use — and that's the part I care most about.

A closed API would decide my stack for me. Open innovation means the model is a swappable piece, not a contract. I can reach for a closed model when it's the best tool, switch to an open-weight one when I want something leaner, run it locally and keep the data on my machine — all on the same harness.

It also gives me control over what I actually spend. Legion surfaces a token budget, so I can watch and cap usage instead of letting a vendor meter every call on their terms. I pick the model, I control the spend, my data stays where I say it does.

That's what open innovation bought me: the freedom to choose the brain and the budget — the platform just handles the work.
